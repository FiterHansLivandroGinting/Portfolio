Logo Sustainability Design

The logo symbolizes a sustainable future driven by AI innovation, blending technology with environmental harmony. A globe at the center represents global interconnectedness and reliance on natural resources and modern technologies. A robot hand gently holding the Earth reflects AI’s role in advancing sustainability without overpowering nature. Circuit-like patterns across continents highlight AI’s global reach, while homes with trees and solar panels emphasize eco-friendly living and environmental preservation. The design envisions a balance between technological progress and environmental stewardship, fostering harmony where AI and sustainability coexist.

Typography Stop Pollution

The typography design delivers a strong message to "Stop Pollution," using dark smoke from factories to form the word "pollution," symbolizing the harmful impact of industrial emissions. A partial globe outline highlights the global scale of the crisis, while the black-and-white composition underscores its severity. The design combines simplicity with impactful visuals to urge collective action against industrial pollution and promote environmental awareness.

Poster Sustainability Journey

This illustration depicts a city focused on sustainable living and eco-friendly transportation. A solar-powered charging station and electric car emphasize the city's commitment to renewable energy. The "Go Eco" sign and recycling bins highlight the importance of eco-friendly practices. In the background, colorful buildings, a windmill, and an electric scooter station showcase a sustainable urban environment. Birds and an airplane in the sky symbolize the balance between nature and modernity.
